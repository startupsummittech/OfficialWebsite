About Startup Summit 2.0

At the heart of the summit are six signature events that bring ideas to life, challenge perspectives, and spark meaningful connections. Each is designed to take you on a journey from shaping your first step, to sharing it with the world. These experiences offer something powerful — the chance to learn and connect.

Pravartan

An immersive Agentic AI workshop that blends creative ideation, problem-solving exercises, and collaborative teamwork under experienced mentors' guidance.

Thalir

A premier startup pitching event giving emerging innovators the stage to present groundbreaking concepts to investors, mentors, and industry leaders.

Tharanga Sangamam

A multidisciplinary panel discussion bringing together voices from academia, industry, and the startup ecosystem for insightful exchanges on innovation.

BharatBuild

A high-energy hackathon for future techies to showcase problem-solving skills and creativity with minimal code.

FinSmart

A practical entrepreneurship ideathon and financial challenge designed specifically for future investors and entrepreneurs.

Pasumai Hackathon

A green tech hackathon focusing on sustainable solutions, environmental innovation, clean technology, and eco-empowerment under TerraNext.
